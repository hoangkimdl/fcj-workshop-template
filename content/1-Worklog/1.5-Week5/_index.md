### Week 1 Objectives:

- Get acquainted with members of First Cloud AI Journey and understand the internship program's rules, regulations, requirements, and weekly learning roadmap.
- Understand the AWS Free Tier 2025 program, including its major changes, account plans, promotional credits, eligibility conditions, and account protection policies.
- Learn the AWS account registration process, explore the AWS Management Console, and understand how to manage account information and promotional credits.
- Understand AWS Budgets and its four budget types, including budget configurations, monitoring periods, usage limits, and cost alert notifications.
- Gain practical experience in creating and configuring AWS Budgets to monitor cloud service costs and resource usage, helping prevent unexpected AWS charges.

### Week 1 Achievements:

- Became familiar with the members of First Cloud AI Journey, the internship program's rules, regulations, requirements, and weekly learning roadmap.

- Gained a comprehensive understanding of the AWS Free Tier 2025 program and its major improvements compared to the previous Free Tier model, particularly for accounts created from July 15, 2025, including:
  - $100 initial credit upon account registration.
  - Additional $100 credit through five specified learning tasks, with $20 associated with each task.
  - Two available account plans: Free Account Plan and Paid Account Plan.
  - Six-month protection period under the Free Account Plan, subject to the program's applicable conditions.
  - Access to more than 30 Always Free services within their applicable free usage limits.
  - Differences in account expiration and automatic closure between the two plans.

- Understood the differences between the Free Account Plan and Paid Account Plan, particularly in terms of:
  - AWS service availability and access restrictions.
  - Promotional credit usage and eligibility.
  - Billing responsibilities when usage exceeds available credits.
  - Account protection and automatic closure conditions.
  - Availability of additional promotional credits and short-term service trials.

- Gained knowledge of the AWS account registration process, including:
  - Providing account information and configuring account credentials.
  - Verifying the registration email.
  - Selecting an appropriate account plan.
  - Providing and verifying payment information.
  - Verifying the registered phone number.
  - Selecting the applicable support plan.

- Became familiar with the AWS Management Console and learned how to navigate the interface, search for AWS services, and access the Billing and Cost Management dashboard.

- Understood how to check promotional credits through the AWS Billing Console, including:
  - Reviewing available credit balances.
  - Checking credit expiration dates.
  - Understanding credit eligibility conditions.
  - Identifying services covered by promotional credits.
  - Monitoring credit consumption to avoid unexpected charges.

- Gained a comprehensive understanding of AWS Budgets and its four main budget types:
  - **Cost Budget:** Monitors actual and forecasted AWS spending against a specified budget amount and sends alerts when configured thresholds are reached or exceeded.
  - **Usage Budget:** Monitors the consumption of selected AWS services against predefined usage limits, such as EC2 running hours.
  - **Reserved Instance (RI) Budget:** Monitors Reserved Instance utilization and coverage to help track the usage of reserved capacity commitments.
  - **Savings Plans Budget:** Monitors Savings Plans utilization and commitments to help track the usage of savings plan benefits.

- Understood the differences between Cost Budget and Usage Budget, particularly that Cost Budget focuses on monetary spending, whereas Usage Budget focuses on the actual consumption of AWS resources.

- Gained knowledge of AWS Budget configuration options, including:
  - Selecting an appropriate budget type.
  - Configuring daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual monitoring periods.
  - Setting fixed or planned budget amounts.
  - Configuring recurring or expiring budgets.
  - Defining budget scopes and the AWS services to be monitored.
  - Setting actual and forecasted cost alert thresholds.
  - Configuring email notifications when budget thresholds are reached or exceeded.

- Became familiar with AWS Budget templates and learned how to use the predefined Monthly Cost Budget template to simplify budget creation and configuration.

- Gained practical experience in creating and configuring an AWS Cost Budget through the AWS Billing and Cost Management console, including:
  - Accessing the Budgets management interface.
  - Selecting an appropriate budget creation method.
  - Configuring a monthly spending limit.
  - Setting alert thresholds and email notifications.
  - Reviewing the created budget and its configuration.

- Learned how to configure and monitor Usage Budgets, particularly how to select the EC2: ELB - Running Hours usage type group to monitor EC2 instance running hours.

- Understood how to review budget status, budget health, and budget history to monitor spending and resource consumption over time.

- Learned the importance of AWS cost management and resource cleanup, including deleting temporary test budgets after completing practical exercises to avoid unnecessary notifications.

- Recognized that AWS Budget alerts are monitoring and notification mechanisms. They do not automatically stop running AWS resources or prevent additional service charges when a budget threshold is exceeded.
